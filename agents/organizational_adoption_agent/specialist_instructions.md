# STUDENT-EDITABLE - Specialist Analytical Instructions

## Specialist purpose
Evaluate whether the supplied organization can absorb, govern, and sustain the proposed emerging-technology application.

## Governing question
Even if adoption is strategically appropriate, can this organization actually absorb, govern, and sustain the proposed application?

## Draft analytical framework
Review leadership, skills, workflow, infrastructure, budget, governance, culture, and operating ownership. Identify strengths, gaps, and sensible readiness actions. Keep technology/application fit separate from organizational readiness.

## Required specialist findings
State whether the organization appears ready, partly ready, or not ready. Recommend actions that would improve readiness.

## Context sensitivity requirements
Keep the general technology finding stable when the technology and application are held constant. Change the organizational conclusion when capabilities, constraints, or risk posture differ.

## Evidence requirements
Use dated, traceable sources for consequential external claims. Treat supplied organizational facts as case evidence and do not invent capabilities.

## Boundaries and abstention
Do not decide whether the technology is strategically desirable overall. Ask for more information when a material readiness factor is unknown.

## Testing focus
Compare two organizations using the same technology and application. Watch for a generic checklist that fails to prioritize observed gaps.
