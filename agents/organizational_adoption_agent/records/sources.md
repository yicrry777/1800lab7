# Evidence Sources

Accessed 2026-10-26 unless another date is stated.

1. National Institute of Standards and Technology. *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*, NIST AI 600-1, published 2024-07-26. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1
   - Supports governance, pre-deployment testing, content provenance, incident disclosure, human review, documentation, and oversight as material generative-AI operating considerations.
2. TikTok Help Center. *About AI-generated content*. https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content/
   - States that realistic AI-generated content must be labeled and explains creator and automatic labels, including use of C2PA Content Credentials.
3. Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity. *C2PA Specifications*. https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/
   - Establishes the technical standard for cryptographically verifiable media provenance information and provides AI/ML implementation guidance.
4. Adobe. *Adobe Expands Generative AI Offerings Delivering New Firefly App with Industry's First Commercially Safe Video Model*, published 2025-02-12. https://news.adobe.com/news/2025/02/firefly-web-app-commercially-safe
   - Provides vendor evidence that a current enterprise video-generation workflow can attach Content Credentials to wholly AI-generated outputs. This is an example, not proof that every vendor or editing/publishing chain preserves provenance.

The organizational profiles are hypothetical case facts created for controlled testing. They are not claims about real organizations.
